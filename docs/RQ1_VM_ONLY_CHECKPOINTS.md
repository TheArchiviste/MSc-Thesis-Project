## Why this assessor candidate

Use `Qwen/Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct`, pinned at `5ede1c97bbab6ce5cda5812749b4c0bdf79b18dd`, in BF16. Its Qwen2ForCausalLM architecture is listed by the installed vLLM 0.6.6.post1 release, and its Transformers requirement is compatible with 4.47.1. This avoids changing the established GPU stack. The original fine-tuned `QCRI/LLMxCPG-Q` checkpoint and its generation settings remain unchanged.

This is a feasibility choice, not proof of grading quality. The actual model load and predeclared 12-anchor/source-proof calibration must pass. Both checkpoints use the Qwen2 architecture; separate weights and excerpt-only requests do not establish independent training data or human validation. The operational independence boundary remains one public excerpt per stateless request, without the private referent or prior conversation.

The official model metadata reports approximately 32.76 billion BF16 parameters, or about 61 GiB for weights before runtime overhead. The proposed assessor uses an 8192-token context, one sequence and eager mode on the single 80 GB H100. Actual fit is untested. The candidate tokenizer has been checked on all 12 public calibration requests: maximum input 1,540 tokens, with 3,072 response tokens reserved. Neither model weights nor grading outputs were downloaded/generated by that CPU check.
